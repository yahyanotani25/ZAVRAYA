## 2. Script


### [0:00] COLD OPEN — 9 millimeter

> **[VISUAL]** Haath ka bohat qareeb ka shot. Angoothe ka nakhun.
> **[SFX]** Gehri, neeche utarti hui goonj.

Ab ek kaam kijiye — khayal mein. Poori zameen ko lijiye. Saare pahaad. Saare samandar. Aath arab insaan. Aur is sab ko daba kar... is nakhun se bhi chhota kar dijiye.

Nau millimeter.

*(pause)*

Aap ne abhi ek black hole bana liya.

Ab us ke paas se roshni bhi wapas nahi aa sakti.

Yeh number — nau millimeter — ek formule se aata hai. Aur woh formula ek fauji afsar ne nikala tha. 1915 mein. Jang ke morche par. Topon ki garaj mein. Chand maheene baad woh mar gaya.

Aur jis shakhs ki theory se yeh formula nikla tha — Einstein — us ne 24 saal baad ek maqala likh kar "sabit" kiya ke aisi cheez kainat mein ban hi nahi sakti.

Einstein phir ghalat tha.

Aur asal raaz jis ne khola — woh Lahore mein paida hone wala ek unnees saal ka ladka tha. Samandar ke beech. Ek jahaz par.

Teen lafz. Khunnas. Jawar. Kunnas.

### [1:35] PART 1 — Ek pathar uchhaliye

Ek pathar oopar uchhaliye. Woh wapas aa jayega.

Zyada zor se uchhaliye — zyada ooncha jayega. Phir bhi wapas.

Lekin ek raftaar aisi hai jis par woh kabhi wapas nahi aayega. Zameen ke liye: 11 kilometer fi second. Isay kehte hain "farar ki raftaar."

> **[FORMULA]** v = √( 2 × G × M ÷ r )

M — sayyare ka wazan. r — us ka radius.

Matlab ek jumle mein: sayyara jitna bhaari — aur jitna CHHOTA — us se bhaagna utna hi mushkil.

Ab is formule se kheliye. Zameen ka wazan wohi rakhiye — lekin usay dabate jaiye. r chhota. Aur chhota. Farar ki raftaar badhti jayegi. Sau kilometer fi second. Hazaar. Ek lakh.

Kitna dabayen — ke yeh raftaar roshni ki raftaar ke barabar ho jaye?

> **[FORMULA]** r = 2 × G × M ÷ c²

> **[TEXT — calculator, ek ek line]** ZAMEEN → 9 millimeter · SURAJ → 3 kilometer

Zameen: nau millimeter. Suraj: teen kilometer.

Us had ke andar se kuch bhi bahar nahi aa sakta. Roshni bhi nahi. Kyunke kainat mein roshni se tez kuch nahi.

Yeh formula 1915 mein mila. Karl Schwarzschild. German fauj ka afsar — Roos ke mahaz par. Einstein ne apni nayi theory abhi ek maheena pehle shaya ki thi. Schwarzschild ne morche mein baith kar us ki equations ka pehla mukammal hal nikaal liya — aur khat mein Einstein ko bhej diya.

Chand maheene baad — beemari se — us ka inteqal ho gaya. Umar: sirf 42 saal.

Aur Einstein? Us ne hal ki ta'reef ki. Lekin us ke natije ko kabhi nahi maana. 1939 mein us ne likha: aisi cheezein haqeeqat mein ban hi nahi saktin.

### [3:05] PART 2 — Lahore ka ladka

19 October 1910. Lahore. Ek bachcha paida hota hai. Naam: Subrahmanyan Chandrasekhar.

1930. Woh unnees saal ka hai. Scholarship mili hai — England ja raha hai. Bombay se jahaz. Do hafte se zyada ka samandari safar.

> **[VISUAL]** 1930 ka bhaap wala jahaz — khula samandar. Arshe par ek dubla patla naujawan, haath mein kaghaz aur qalam.

Aur us ke paas karne ko kuch nahi. Sirf kaghaz, qalam — aur ek sawal: jab sitare ka eindhan khatam ho jaye — to usay apne hi wazan ke neeche pichakne se kaun rokta hai?

Us waqt ka jawab tha: sitara sikud kar ek chhota, ghana gola ban jata hai. Aur bas. Hamesha ke liye.

Chandra ne hisaab mein ek cheez daali jo sab bhool gaye the: Einstein ki relativity.

Aur jahaz ke arshe par — samandar ke beech — usay ek number mila.

> **[FORMULA]** 1.4 × SURAJ

Agar marta hua sitara Suraj se 1.4 guna bhaari ho — to woh taqat bhi jawab de jati hai jo usay thaame hue thi. Woh pichakta chala jayega.

1935. London. Chandra apna hisaab duniya ke bade astronomers ke saamne rakhta hai. Aur us ke foran baad — us daur ka sab se mashhoor astronomer, Sir Arthur Eddington — khada hota hai. Aur bhari mehfil mein kehta hai:

"Mere khayal mein fitrat ka koi qanoon hona chahiye — jo sitare ko aisi behooda harkat se roke."

Baat hansi mein uda di gayi.

Chandra khamosh raha. Kuch arse baad America chala gaya.

48 saal baad — 1983 mein — usay Nobel Prize mila. Usi kaam par.

Aur aaj NASA ki ek khalai doorbeen ka naam hai: "Chandra."

### [4:30] PART 3 — Ek chamach sitara

To 1.4 se bhaari sitare ke saath hota kya hai?

Pehla darja: atom hi toot jate hain. Electron protons ke andar dhans jate hain. Poora sitara ek hi cheez ka ban jata hai: neutron. Suraj se bhaari — lekin sirf ek shehar jitna. Bees kilometer.

Is ka ek chamach bhar lijiye.

> **[TEXT]** 1 CHAMACH = 2 ARAB TON

Do arab ton. Yaani zameen par maujood tamam insaanon ke kul wazan ka... chaar guna.

Ek chamach mein.

Aur yeh sirf hisaab nahi. 1967. Cambridge. Chaubees saal ki ek talibah — Jocelyn Bell — ek radio telescope ke kaghzi record dekh rahi hai. Rozana tees meter kaghaz.

Us mein usay ek ajeeb nishan milta hai. Ek "dhadkan." Har ek second aur ek tihai ke baad.

> **[SFX]** Tik... tik... tik... — theek 1.33 second ke waqfe se, chaar baar.

Itni ba-qaida — ke team ne mazaq mein us ka naam rakh diya: "LGM." Little Green Men. Chhote sabz aadmi.

Woh koi makhlooq nahi thi. Woh tezi se ghoomta hua neutron sitara tha — jis ki shu'a har chakkar par zameen se takrati thi. Jaise samandar ke kinare roshni ka minar.

1974 mein is daryaft par Nobel Prize mila. Us ke ustad ko. Jocelyn ko nahi.

Aur Quran:

> **[TILAWAT · At-Tariq 86:1-3]**
>
> وَٱلسَّمَآءِ وَٱلطَّارِقِ
>
> وَمَآ أَدْرَىٰكَ مَا ٱلطَّارِقُ
>
> ٱلنَّجْمُ ٱلثَّاقِبُ

"Qasam hai aasman ki — aur raat ko aane wale ki. Aur tumhein kya maloom woh raat ko aane wala kya hai? Woh chamakta hua sitara — jo cheer deta hai."

"Tariq." Arabi mein us ko kehte hain jo raat ke waqt aa kar darwaza khatkhataye.

Insaaf ki baat. Mufassireen ne is se koi bhi tez roshan sitara muraad liya hai — jis ki roshni andhere ko cheer de. "Khatkhatane wale" sitare se is ko jodna hamari mumasilat hai — tafsir nahi.

Lekin woh sawal dekhiye jo ayat khud poochti hai: "tumhein kya maloom?"

### [6:20] PART 4 — Jab kuch bhi na rok sake

Aur agar bacha hua sitara taqreeban teen Suraj se bhi bhaari ho?

To neutron bhi nahi rok sakte.

Sitara apne hi andar girta chala jata hai. Us "teen kilometer" wali had se bhi andar. Aur phir... bahar ki kainat se us ka rishta kat jata hai.

Black hole.

Aur us ke kinare par waqt ke saath wohi hota hai jo is channel ki "Waqt ka Raaz" wali video mein tafseel se hai. Zyada gravity — sust waqt.

> **[FORMULA]** t = t₀ ÷ √( 1 − r_s ÷ r )

Kinare se zara sa bahar: aap ka ek ghanta — door baithe logon ke das ghante. Aur qareeb: sau ghante. Aur theek kinare par? Formule ka neeche wala hissa sifar ho jata hai.

Bahar se dekhne wale ke liye — waqt ruk jata hai.

"Yeh sab kaghaz ki baatein hain." Nahi.

2015. Do black holes — ek arab tees crore noori saal door — aapas mein takrate hain. Teen Suraj jitna maada — ek second ke paanchwen hisse mein — poora ka poora energy ban jata hai. E = mc². Us ek lamhe mein un ki taqat... nazar aane wali poori kainat ke tamam sitaron se zyada thi.

Aur zameen par ek machine ne us ki larzish pakad li.

2019. Insaan ne pehli baar black hole ki tasveer li.

> **[VISUAL]** Event Horizon Telescope ki asli tasveer (M87*): narangi halqa, beech mein kaala saaya.

Saadhe chhe arab Suraj ke barabar. Us ki had — Pluto ke madaar se bhi kai guna badi.

Aur 2022 mein doosri tasveer. Hamari apni galaxy ke bilkul beech mein. Tentaalees lakh Suraj.

Aap is waqt us ke gird ghoom rahe hain. "Falak" wali video ka counter yaad hai? 220 kilometer fi second. Wohi.

### [7:55] PART 5 — Teen lafz

Ab un teen lafzon par wapas aaiye.

> **[TEXT — teen lafz, ek ek kar ke]** بِٱلْخُنَّسِ · ٱلْجَوَارِ · ٱلْكُنَّسِ

"Khunnas." Is ki jad ka matlab hai: peeche hat jana. Sikud jana. Chhup jana. Yehi lafz Surah An-Nas mein hai — "al-khannas": woh jo waswasa daal kar peeche hat jata hai.

"Jawar." Chalne wale. Behne wale.

"Kunnas." Is ke do ma'ni hain. Ek: hiran ka apni panaah-gah mein chhup jana. Aur doosra: jhaadu dena. Arabi mein jhaadu ko "miknasa" kehte hain. Aur aaj ki Arabi mein vacuum cleaner ka naam hai: "miknasa kahruba'iyya." Bijli ka jhaadu.

Ab insaaf ki baat — aur yeh zaroori hai.

Sahaba aur tabi'een ne in ayaton se kya samjha? Hazrat Ali (raziyallahu anhu) se manqool hai: yeh woh sitare hain jo din mein chhup jate hain aur raat ko zahir hote hain. Doosre mufassireen ne kaha: yeh paanch sayyare hain — jo seedha chalte chalte peeche hat'ne lagte hain. Aur yeh bhi sachi falakiyaat hai: Mars har do saal baad kuch hafton ke liye aasman mein ulta chalta nazar aata hai.

To hum yeh nahi keh rahe ke is ayat ki tafsir "black hole" hai.

Hum sirf teen lafz aap ke saamne rakh rahe hain — aur ek cheez.

Woh jo sikud kar chhup gaya. Jo nazar nahi aata. Jo galaxy mein chal raha hai. Aur jo qareeb aane wali har cheez ko... samet leta hai.

Faisla aap ka.

### [9:20] PART 6 — Jahan formula toot jata hai

Aur aakhri baat — jo mere nazdeek sab se badi hai.

Black hole ke bilkul markaz mein kya hai?

Physics ki equations wahan ek hi jawab deti hain: "la-mehdood." Infinity.

Aur science ki zubaan mein "infinity" ka ek hi matlab hota hai: "hum nahi jaante." Yahan hamara ilm khatam hota hai.

Kainat ki sab se roshan cheez — ek sitara — mar kar kainat ki sab se tareek cheez ban jati hai.

> **[TILAWAT · Al-Mursalat 77:8]**
>
> فَإِذَا ٱلنُّجُومُ طُمِسَتْ

"Phir jab sitare be-noor kar diye jayenge."

> **[TILAWAT · Al-Qasas 28:88]**
>
> كُلُّ شَىْءٍ هَالِكٌ إِلَّا وَجْهَهُۥ

"Har cheez fana hone wali hai — siwaye us ki zaat ke."

*(pause)*

Aaj raat ka mission.

> **[TEXT]** AAJ RAAT KA MISSION 🌙

Sardiyon ki raat hai — to mashriq ki taraf dekhiye. Teen sitare ek seedhi line mein. Un ke oopar, baayin taraf — ek surkh sitara. Us ka naam Arabi hai: "Yad-ul-Jauza." Duniya usay Betelgeuse kehti hai.

Yeh sitara Suraj se sainkdon guna bada hai — aur apni zindagi ke aakhri daur mein hai. Kisi bhi din — aaj raat, ya ek lakh saal baad — yeh phat jayega.

Aap ek marte hue sitare ko dekh rahe honge.

Aur ek din — bohat door mustaqbil mein — aakhri sitara bhi bujh jayega. Kainat mein sirf black holes bachenge.

Phir kya hoga?

Science ke paas is ke chaar jawab hain. Aur un mein se ek — jise scientists pachees saal tak radd karte rahe — 2025 mein achanak wapas aa gaya.

"Jis din Hum aasman ko lapet denge..."

Aakhri video. Kainat ka aakhri lamha. Screen par aa chuki hai.
