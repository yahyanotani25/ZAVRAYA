Oonchi wali ghadi... tez chali.

Waqt har jagah ek raftaar se nahi chalta.

Ab yeh suniye. 1400 saal pehle — jab duniya ke paas ghadi tak nahi thi — inkaar karne wale Rasulullah ﷺ ka mazaq udate the: "Kahan hai woh azaab jis se darate ho? Jaldi lao!"

### [1:30] PART 1 — Newton ki ghadi, Einstein ka sawal

1687. Newton apni mashhoor kitab mein likhta hai: "Mutlaq, sachcha aur riyazi waqt — apni fitrat se, kisi bahari cheez se be-niyaz — ek-saan behta hai."

Yaani kainat mein ek hi "master clock" hai. Mera ek second, aapka ek second, Mars ka ek second — sab barabar.

Aur sach poochiye to hamari aqal bhi yehi kehti hai. Do sau saal tak kisi ne is par shak nahi kiya.

Phir 1905 mein patent office ka ek 26 saala clerk aaya. Naam: Albert Einstein. Usne ek bachchon jaisa sawal poocha: "Agar main roshni ki kiran ke saath saath bhaagun — to mujhe kya nazar aayega?"

Is ek sawal ne physics ki bunyaad hila di. Jawab yeh nikla: roshni ki raftaar har dekhne wale ke liye ek hi rehti hai. To phir kisi aur cheez ko badalna padega.

Aur jo cheez badalti hai — woh waqt hai.

Bas do usool yaad rakhiye. Sirf do.

> **[TEXT]** 1 — TEZ RAFTAAR = SUST WAQT · 2 — ZYADA GRAVITY = SUST WAQT

Ek: jitni tez raftaar — utna sust waqt.

Do: jitni zyada gravity — utna sust waqt.

Aur yeh ghadi ki kharabi nahi. Waqt khud sust ho jata hai. Aapka dil dheere dhadakta hai. Aapke baal dheere badhte hain. Aap dheere boodhe hote hain. Aur aapko khud... kuch mehsoos nahi hota.

### [2:45] PART 2 — Saboot aapki jeb mein hai

"Yeh to sirf theory hai." — Nahi.

October 1971. Do scientists — Hafele aur Keating — chaar atomic ghadiyan uthate hain, aam musafir jahazon mein baithte hain, aur duniya ka poora chakkar lagate hain. Ek baar mashriq ki taraf. Ek baar maghrib ki taraf.

Wapas aa kar zameen par rakhi ghadi se milate hain.

Ghadiyan alag alag waqt bata rahi thin.

Mashriq wali 59 nanosecond peeche. Maghrib wali 273 nanosecond aage. Taqreeban utna hi — jitna Einstein ki equations ne pehle se bata diya tha.

Aur ab apni jeb mein aaiye. Aapka phone. Google Maps. Foodpanda ka rider. Careem ki gaadi.

> **[VISUAL]** Phone map par neela nuqta. Camera upar uthta hai — zameen — satellites ka halqa.

GPS ke satellites 20,000 kilometer upar hain aur taqreeban 14,000 kilometer fi ghanta ki raftaar se ud rahe hain.

Raftaar ki wajah se un ki ghadiyan har roz 7 microsecond sust chalti hain. Lekin wahan gravity kamzor hai — is liye 45 microsecond tez. Hisaab lagaiye: har roz 38 microsecond ka farq.

38 microsecond. Kuch bhi nahi, hai na?

Agar engineers yeh farq theek na karein — to aapka GPS har roz taqreeban 10 kilometer ghalat hota jaye. Ek hafte baad aapka rider khana le kar doosre shehar pohanch chuka hoga.

Yaani jab bhi aap kisi ko apni location bhejte hain — aap sabit kar rahe hote hain ke waqt har jagah ek jaisa nahi chalta.

2020 mein Japan ke scientists ne Tokyo Skytree tower ke neeche aur 450 meter upar ghadiyan rakhin. Upar wali har roz 4 nanosecond aage.

Aur 2022 mein America mein yeh farq sirf EK MILLIMETER ki oonchai par naap liya gaya.

To haan. Aapka sar waqai aapke pairon se zyada boodha hai. Poori zindagi mein farq ek second ke das lakhwen hisse se bhi kam — lekin hai.

### [4:30] PART 3 — 1,000... ya 50,000?

Ab us ayat par wapas chaliye.

"...ek din — hazaar saal jaisa — us ginti se jo TUM ginte ho."

Do tukdon par ghaur kijiye.

"'Inda Rabbika" — "tumhare Rab ke HAAN." Yaani ek aur paimana.

"Mimma ta'uddun" — "jo TUM ginte ho." Yaani tumhari ginti — tumhari hai.

Ayat saaf keh rahi hai: waqt ka paimana ek nahi.

Aur Quran yahan rukta nahi. Surah Al-Ma'arij:

> **[TILAWAT · Al-Ma'arij 70:4]**
>
> تَعْرُجُ ٱلْمَلَٰٓئِكَةُ وَٱلرُّوحُ إِلَيْهِ فِى يَوْمٍۢ كَانَ مِقْدَارُهُۥ خَمْسِينَ أَلْفَ سَنَةٍۢ

"Farishte aur Rooh uski taraf chadhte hain — ek aise din mein jiski miqdaar pachaas hazaar saal hai."

Yahan hazaar nahi. Pachaas hazaar.

Sadiyon tak aitraz karne walon ne kaha: "Dekho — tazaad! Kahin 1,000, kahin 50,000!"

Aaj relativity padhne wala koi bhi talib-e-ilm is aitraz par muskura dega. Kyunke relativity ka pehla sabaq hi yehi hai: waqt ka koi ek rate hota hi nahi. Halaat badle — rate badla.

Aur Hazrat Ibn Abbas (raziyallahu anhu) ne 1400 saal pehle yehi jawab diya tha: yeh do alag din hain.

Ab ek chhota sa hisaab — sirf andaza lagane ke liye. Einstein ke formule ke mutabiq, agar koi cheez roshni ki raftaar ke 99.9999999996 feesad par safar kare — to uska ek din zameen ke hazaar saal ke barabar hoga.

> **[TEXT]** v = 99.9999999996% × c → 1 din = 1,000 saal

Hum yeh nahi keh rahe ke ayat mein kisi ki speed likhi hai. Hum sirf yeh keh rahe hain: jo baat saatwin sadi mein "namumkin" lagti thi — aaj physics ki har textbook mein likhi hai.

### [6:20] PART 4 — Khana taaza, gadha haddiyan

Aur ab Quran ka woh waqia — jo mere nazdeek waqt ke baare mein sab se hairat-angez bayan hai.

Surah Al-Baqarah, ayat 259.

Ek shakhs — mashhoor qaul ke mutabiq Hazrat Uzair (alayhis-salam) — ek ujdi hui basti se guzarte hain. Chhatein giri hui. Sannata. Dil mein ek sawal uthta hai: "Allah is basti ko marne ke baad kaise zinda karega?"

Allah unhein wahin maut de deta hai. Sau saal. Phir uthata hai — aur poochta hai:

>

"Kitni der thehre?" — "Ek din... ya din ka kuch hissa." — "Nahi. Tum sau saal thehre ho."

Aur ab yeh jumla. Lafz ba lafz suniye:

> **[TILAWAT · Al-Baqarah 2:259]**

> فَٱنظُرْ إِلَىٰ طَعَامِكَ وَشَرَابِكَ لَمْ يَتَسَنَّهْ ۖ وَٱنظُرْ إِلَىٰ حِمَارِكَ

"Apne khane aur peene ki cheez ko dekho — us par saal guzre hi nahi. Aur apne gadhe ko dekho."

> **[VISUAL]** Split screen: baayin taraf taaza khana, bhaap uthti hui. Daayin taraf — usi jagah — ek dhancha.

Khana — jo ek din mein kharab ho jata hai — sau saal baad taaza.

Gadha — jo usi jagah khada tha — gal kar haddiyan.

Ek hi jagah. Ek hi dhoop. Ek hi hawa.

Lekin khane par sau saal guzre hi nahi. Gadhe par poore sau saal guzar gaye. Aur us shakhs ke apne ehsaas mein — "din ka kuch hissa."

Teen cheezein. Teen alag waqt.

Aur Arabi lafz dekhiye: "lam yatasannah." Lughat ke bohat se imamon ke nazdeek yeh "sanah" se bana hai — yaani "saal." Lafzi matlab: "us par saal nahi guzre."

Yeh ek mo'jiza tha — hum isay physics se "explain" nahi kar rahe. Lekin dekhiye is ek ayat ne insaan ko kya sikhaya: waqt koi aisa darya nahi jo sab par ek jaisa behta ho. Waqt makhlooq hai — Allah ke hukm ka paband. Chahe to ek cheez par rok de, doosri par guzaar de.

1300 saal baad Einstein ne isi se milti julti baat equation mein likhi: har cheez ka apna waqt hota hai.

### [8:25] PART 5 — "Teen sau... aur nau"

Aur phir Surah Kahf.

Chand naujawan — apna imaan bachane ke liye — ek ghaar mein panaah lete hain. Allah unhein sula deta hai. Jab aankh khulti hai — wohi sawal: "Kitni der thehre?" Aur wohi jawab: "Ek din... ya din ka kuch hissa."

Asal mein kitna soye the? Ayat 25:

> **[TILAWAT · Al-Kahf 18:25]**
>
> وَلَبِثُوا۟ فِى كَهْفِهِمْ ثَلَٰثَ مِا۟ئَةٍۢ سِنِينَ وَٱزْدَادُوا۟ تِسْعًۭا

"Aur woh apne ghaar mein teen sau saal rahe — aur nau aur badha diye."

Is jumle ko dobara suniye. "Teen sau saal... aur nau badha diye."

Seedha "teen sau nau" kyun nahi kaha? Arabi mein 309 kehna koi mushkil nahi. Yeh "300 — aur 9 ka izafa" kyun?

Ab apna phone uthaiye. Calculator kholiye. Sach mein — video pause kar lijiye.

Teen sau nau. Point do.

300 shamsi saal — barabar 309 qamari saal.

Ek hi muddat. Do calendar. Aur ayat ne dono ginti ek jumle mein rakh di: "teen sau... aur nau ka izafa."

Aur yeh aaj ki koi "nayi daryaft" nahi. Imam Ibn Kathir ne 700 saal pehle apni tafsir mein yehi likha: shamsi hisaab se 300, qamari hisaab se 309 — har sau saal mein teen saal ka farq.

Ab sochiye. Arab chaand ka calendar chalate the. Rome suraj ka. Aur ek ummi ﷺ — jinhone kabhi kisi maktab mein qadam nahi rakha — do calendaron ka teen sadiyon ka farq... aur phir us farq ko itni nafasat se ek jumle mein?

### [10:35] PART 6 — Aapki poori zindagi: "din ka kuch hissa"

Ab ek cheez par ghaur kiya aapne?

Uzair (alayhis-salam) ne kaha: "Ek din — ya din ka kuch hissa."

Ashab-e-Kahf ne kaha: "Ek din — ya din ka kuch hissa."

Quran yehi jumla — bilkul yehi — ek teesri jagah bhi laata hai. Aur wahan yeh jumla bolne wale... hum honge. Aap aur main.

Qayamat ka din. Allah poochega:

> **[TILAWAT · Al-Mu'minun 23:112-113]**
>
> قَٰلَ كَمْ لَبِثْتُمْ فِى ٱلْأَرْضِ عَدَدَ سِنِينَ
>
> قَالُوا۟ لَبِثْنَا يَوْمًا أَوْ بَعْضَ يَوْمٍۢ

"Tum zameen mein ginti ke kitne saal rahe?"

"Woh kahenge: ek din... ya din ka kuch hissa."

Saath saal. Sattar saal. Assi saal. Padhai, naukri, shaadi, bachche, pareshaniyan, khwab...

"Din ka kuch hissa."

Aap ne khud mehsoos kiya hoga. Bachpan mein garmiyon ki chhuttiyan kitni lambi lagti thin? Aur ab? Ramzan aata hai — aur chala jata hai. Pichhle das saal... kahan gaye?

### [11:35] PART 7 — "Agar Allah ko pehle se pata hai..."

Aur ab woh sawal.

"Agar Allah ko pehle se pata hai ke main kal kya karunga — to main azaad kaise hua? Aur agar azaad nahi — to hisaab kis baat ka?"

Yeh sawal ek ghalat-fehmi par khada hai. Aur woh ghalat-fehmi sirf do lafzon mein chhupi hai: "pehle se."

"Pehle" aur "baad" — yeh un ke lafz hain jo waqt ke andar rehte hain. Aap aur main waqt ke andar hain — jaise machhli paani ke andar. Hum sirf "ab" dekh sakte hain. Ek ek lamha.

Lekin waqt khud ek makhlooq hai. Pehli video yaad kijiye — waqt kainat ke saath paida hua. Aur khaliq apni makhlooq ke andar qaid nahi hota.

> **[TILAWAT · Al-Hadid 57:3]**
>
> هُوَ ٱلْأَوَّلُ وَٱلْءَاخِرُ

"Wohi Awwal hai — aur wohi Akhir."

Ek misaal. Kal raat Pakistan ka match tha. Aaj aap us ki recording dekh rahe hain. Aur aap ko natija pata hai. Aap jaante hain ke aakhri over mein Babar chhakka maarega.

Sawal: kya aapke jaan'ne ne Babar se woh chhakka lagwaya?

Nahi. Babar ne apni marzi se balla ghumaya. Aap sirf us maqam par khade hain — jahan se poora match ek saath nazar aata hai.

Misaal adhoori hai — Allah ki koi misaal nahi. Lekin itni baat samajh aa jati hai: Allah ke liye aapka "kal" — mustaqbil hai hi nahi. Uska ilm aapko majboor nahi karta. Uska ilm bas... kaamil hai.

Aur ajeeb baat: relativity se kainat ki jo tasveer nikalti hai — bohat se physicists usay "block universe" kehte hain. Maazi, haal, mustaqbil — sab ek saath "maujood."

Einstein ne apne sab se qareebi dost ki wafat par us ke ghar walon ko likha: "Hum jaise log — jo physics par yaqeen rakhte hain — jaante hain ke maazi, haal aur mustaqbil ka farq sirf ek ziddi waham hai."

Aur Rasulullah ﷺ ne 1400 saal pehle Allah ka farman sunaya — Bukhari aur Muslim ki hadith-e-qudsi hai:

"Ibn-e-Adam zamane ko bura kehta hai — halanke zamana Main hoon. Raat aur din Mere haath mein hain."

Yaani waqt ka maalik, waqt ko palatne wala — Woh hai.

### [13:35] PART 8 — Pighalta hua sarmaya

To waqt kya hai?

Newton ne kaha: ek mutlaq darya, jo sab ke liye ek jaisa behta hai. Ghalat nikla.

Einstein ne kaha: ek lachakdaar cheez — jo raftaar aur gravity se khinchti aur sikudti hai. Sahi nikla — aur roz aapke phone mein sabit hota hai.

Aur Quran ne — in dono se sadiyon pehle — kaha: tumhara hazaar saal kisi aur paimane ka ek din hai. Tumhare sau saal — "din ka kuch hissa." Aur tumhari poori zindagi...

> **[TILAWAT · Al-'Asr 103:1-2]**
>
> وَٱلْعَصْرِ ۝ إِنَّ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ لَفِى خُسْرٍ

"Zamane ki qasam. Beshak insaan khasare mein hai."

Allah ne waqt ki qasam khaayi hai.

Tafsir-e-Kabir mein Imam Razi ne ek buzurg ka qaul naqal kiya hai: "Maine Surah Asr ka matlab ek barf bechne wale se seekha. Woh bazaar mein awaaz laga raha tha: 'Logo! Us shakhs par reham karo — jiska sarmaya pighal raha hai!'"
