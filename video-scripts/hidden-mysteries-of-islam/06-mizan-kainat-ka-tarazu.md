### [1:20] PART 1 — Pehla number: 36 sifar

> **[FORMULA]** F = G × (m₁ × m₂) ÷ r²

Yeh Newton ka gravity ka qanoon hai. Dariye mat — yeh sirf ek jumla hai:

Do cheezein ek doosre ko kheenchti hain. Jitna zyada un ka wazan — utni zyada kheench. Jitna zyada faasla — utni kam. Aur faasla dugna karo to kheench chauthai reh jati hai — is liye neeche "r square."

Aur yeh "G"? Yeh gravity ki taqat ka number hai. Aur yeh number... hairat-angez had tak chhota hai.

Kitna chhota? Ek tajurba kijiye. Abhi.

Fridge par laga koi chhota sa maqnatees lijiye — aur us se ek pin uthaiye.

> **[VISUAL]** Ek chhota fridge-magnet pin ko utha leta hai. Camera peeche hat'ta hai — neeche poori zameen.

Dekha?

Aur ek rupay ke sikke jitna maqnatees... us se jeet gaya.

Yeh hai gravity. Kainat ki sab se kamzor taqat.

Ab hisaab. Bijli ki taqat ka formula hoo-ba-hoo aisa hi hai:

> **[FORMULA]** F = k × (q₁ × q₂) ÷ r²

Bas wazan ki jagah charge — aur G ki jagah k.

Do protons lijiye. Dono formule un par lagaiye. Bijli unhein dhakelti hai. Gravity unhein kheenchti hai. Ab ek ko doosre par taqseem kijiye.

> **[TEXT — sifar ek ek kar ke screen par bhagte hue]** BIJLI ÷ GRAVITY = 1,000,000,000,000,000,000,000,000,000,000,000,000

"To kya hua?" — aap kahenge.

Yeh hua: Martin Rees ne hisaab lagaya ke agar is mein 36 ki jagah sirf 30 sifar hote — yaani gravity zara si mazboot hoti — to sitare itne chhote hote aur itni jaldi jal kar khatam ho jate ke zindagi ko waqt hi na milta. Aur gravity itni sakht hoti ke keede-makode se badi koi makhlooq apne hi wazan ke neeche kuchli jati.

36 sifar. 30 nahi.

### [3:00] PART 2 — Doosra number: 0.007

Doosra number aap jaante hain. 0.007.

Yeh batata hai ke atom ka markaz — nucleus — kitni mazbooti se juda hua hai.

Agar 0.006 hota: proton aur neutron aapas mein tik hi na paate. Seedhi ka pehla danda hi toot jata. Kainat mein sirf hydrogen hoti. Na koi chemistry. Na koi zindagi.

Agar 0.008 hota: do protons seedhe aapas mein jud jate — aur kainat ke pehle chand minute mein hi hydrogen jal kar khatam ho jati. Hydrogen nahi — to paani nahi. Paani ka naam hi H₂O hai.

0.007. Na 6. Na 8.

### [3:35] PART 3 — Teesra number: woh aadmi jo carbon dhoondne gaya

Teesre number ke saath ek kahani hai.

1953. California. Ek angrez scientist — Fred Hoyle — ek lab mein daakhil hota hai.

Hoyle woh shakhs hai jis ne "Big Bang" ka naam rakha tha — tanz ke taur par. Us ko yeh khayal hi na-pasand tha ke kainat ki koi ibtida ho. Us ke nazdeek is se mazhab ki boo aati thi.

Hoyle ek masle mein phansa hua tha: carbon.

Aap carbon se bane hain. Aap ka har khuliya. Lekin sitare ke andar carbon ban'na taqreeban namumkin hai.

> **[FORMULA]** ⁴He + ⁴He + ⁴He → ¹²C

Carbon ke liye helium ke TEEN zarron ko ek saath takrana hota hai. Do takrate hain — to jo cheez banti hai woh itni na-paidaar hai ke ek second ke ek crore arabwen hisse mein toot jati hai.

Itne se waqt mein teesra zarra aaye, takraye — aur chipak bhi jaye? Hisaab ke mutabiq carbon itni kam banni chahiye thi... ke hum hote hi nahi.

Lekin hum hain.

To Hoyle ne ulta socha. Us ne kaha: carbon ke andar zaroor ek khaas "sur" hoga — ek khaas energy — jahan yeh teeno zarre bilkul fit baith jate hain. Jaise jhoola: aap theek waqt par dhakka dein — to thoda sa zor bhi usay aasman tak le jata hai. Isay kehte hain resonance.

Us ne number bhi bata diya: taqreeban 7.7.

Lab walon ne kaha: aisa koi level maloom nahi.

Hoyle ne kaha: dhoondo. Woh hoga. Kyunke main yahan khada hoon.

Unhone dhoonda.

> **[TEXT]** CARBON-12 — 7.65 MeV ✓ · [SFX: ek saaf "ting"]

7.65. Woh wahin tha — jahan Hoyle ne kaha tha.

Aur ek cheez aur. Agar oxygen mein bhi aisa hi "sur" hota — to saara carbon foran jal kar oxygen ban jata. Oxygen ka level... zara sa neeche hai. Bas itna neeche ke carbon bach jaye.

Carbon ban bhi gaya. Bach bhi gaya.

Hoyle — mazhab ka wohi sakht naqid — baad mein likhta hai:

> **[TEXT — Hoyle ki tasveer ke saath, lafz ba lafz]** "...a superintellect has monkeyed with physics, as well as with chemistry and biology..." — Fred Hoyle, 1981

"Haqaiq ko aam aqal se dekhein — to lagta hai ke kisi super-intellect ne physics ke saath chhed-chhaad ki hai. Aur chemistry aur biology ke saath bhi."

### [5:35] PART 4 — Chautha number: 120 sifar

Chautha number physics ke liye sab se sharmnaak hai.

Khaali khalaa — khaali nahi hai. Us mein ek energy hai jo kainat ko phaila rahi hai. Pehli video yaad kijiye: "Hum wus'at dene wale hain."

Physics ne hisaab lagaya ke yeh energy kitni HONI chahiye. Phir naap kar dekha ke kitni HAI.

Hisaab aur haqeeqat mein farq nikla... 120 sifar ka.

> **[TEXT]** HISAAB ÷ NAAP = 10¹²⁰

Khud physicists isay "physics ki tareekh ki sab se buri peshgoi" kehte hain.

Aur agar yeh energy zara si bhi zyada hoti — to kainat itni tezi se phailti ke gas kabhi ikatthi ho kar ek galaxy na bana paati. Na sitare. Na loha. Na aap.

120 sifar ka matlab samajhiye. Poori kainat mein kul atom kitne hain? Ek ke aage 80 sifar.

To yeh aisa hai — jaise aap ke saamne ek kainat nahi, balke ek ke aage 40 sifar jitni kainaatein rakhi hon. Aur un tamam kainaaton ke tamam atoms mein se... aap ko aankh band kar ke ek khaas atom uthana ho.

Pehli hi baar mein.

### [6:40] PART 5 — Paanchwan number: jo likha nahi ja sakta

Aur paanchwan number — jis ka main ne wada kiya tha.

Sir Roger Penrose. Nobel Prize, 2020.

Us ne ek sawal poocha: kainat ki ibtida kitni "tarteeb" mein thi? Kyunke agar ibtida zara bhi be-tarteeb hoti — to na sitare bante, na zindagi.

Phir us ne hisaab lagaya: ittefaq se itni ba-tarteeb ibtida milne ka imkaan kya hai?

Ek mein se... das ki taqat, das ki taqat 123.

> **[FORMULA]** 1 ÷ 10^(10¹²³)

Yeh number likha nahi ja sakta. Penrose khud kehta hai: agar aap kainat ke har zarre par ek sifar likhein — har proton par, har neutron par — to bhi sifar kam pad jayenge.

Aur Penrose mazhabi aadmi nahi hai. Lekin is nishane ke liye us ne apni kitab mein jo lafz likha — woh suniye: "the Creator's aim." Khaliq ka nishana.

**Comments mein yeh aitraz aayenge — tayyar jawab:**

- **"Yeh 'God of the gaps' hai — kal science wajah dhoond legi."** — Mumkin hai kal koi gehri theory in numbers ko samjha de. Lekin phir sawal us theory par chala jayega: woh theory aisi kyun hai ke zindagi mumkin ho? Daleel "hum nahi jaante" par nahi — "jo hum jaante hain" par khadi hai.
- **"Kainat ka 99.99% hissa zindagi ke liye mohlik hai — yeh kaisi fine-tuning?"** — Fine-tuning ka matlab yeh nahi ke har jagah zindagi ho; matlab yeh hai ke qawaneen aise hain ke kahin bhi zindagi mumkin ho saki. Bade size aur lambi umar ke baghair carbon banta hi nahi.
- **"Rees khud to Khuda ko nahi maanta."** — Durust, aur script ne kahin nahi kaha ke maanta hai. Rees multiverse ko tarjeeh deta hai. Hum ne us ke numbers liye hain — nateeja dekhne wale par chhoda hai.
