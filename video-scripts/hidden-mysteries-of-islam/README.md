## Har file mein kya hai

1. **Packaging** — 3 title options, thumbnail ka concept, description ki pehli 2 lines.
2. **Script** — timestamps ke saath, lafz ba lafz narration + editor ke liye cues.
3. **Fact-check sheet** — har daawe ka hawala aur uska darja, plus comments mein aane wale aitrazaat ke tayyar jawab.
4. **Pinned comment.**

## Script kaise padhein

- **Seedhi lines** = narration. Yehi bolna hai, lafz ba lafz.
- **`>` se shuru hone wali lines** = editor ke liye. Bolni nahi hain:
  - `[VISUAL]` screen par kya dikhega · `[TEXT]` screen par likha hua · `[SFX]` awaaz/music ka cue
  - `[TILAWAT]` ayat ki tilawat (qari ki recording ya khud) — Arabi matn saath likha hai
  - `[✂️ OPTIONAL]` yahan se yahan tak kaat sakte hain
- **CAPS** = us lafz par awaaz mein zor. ***(pause)*** = 1–2 second ki khamoshi.
- **Timestamps** 160 lafz/minute ki raftaar par nikale gaye hain (tilawat aur pause shamil). Aapki raftaar ke hisaab se thoda aage peeche honge.

## Retention ke liye script mein kya rakha gaya hai — editing mein yeh mat kaatiye

- **Pehle 5 second mein jhatka.** Salam aur channel ka naam hook ke *baad* (har script mein jagah nishan-zad hai).
- **Shuru mein 3 wade** — aur sab se bada wada sab se aakhir mein poora hota hai.
- **Har 60–90 second par naya sawal** ya "lekin rukiye" — taake dekhne wale ke dimagh mein hamesha ek khula sawal rahe.
- **Callbacks:** kabootar (Video 1), lafz "aadat" (Video 2), "din ka kuch hissa" (Video 3), Taj Mahal (Video 4) — yeh jumle do baar aate hain; doosri baar hi asal maza deti hai.
- **"Insaaf ki baat" wale jumle.** Jahan koi cheez tashreeh hai, script khud keh deti hai. Yeh jumle video ko kamzor nahi karte — yehi aapko un channels se alag karte hain jin ke daawe comments mein toot jate hain.

## Sehat (accuracy) ke teen usool

1. **Arabi matn haath se type nahi kiya gaya.** Har ayat Tanzil ke Uthmani matn se seedhi uthayi gayi hai (machine se, taake zer-zabar ki ghalti na ho). Phir bhi upload se pehle screen par lagne wali har ayat ko mushaf se ek nazar mila lein.
2. **Har daawe ka hawala** fact-check sheet mein hai, darje ke saath: ✅ pakka · ⚠️ tashreeh ya ek se zyada raaye · 🗣️ mashhoor riwayat.
3. **Numbers khud check kiye gaye hain:** 300 shamsi = 309.2 qamari saal; GPS ka 38 microsecond; "kullun fi falak" ka ulta-seedha ek hona; "yawm" ki 365 ginti (usool ke saath) — sab calculator/code se.

Do jagah aap ko khud dekhna hai: Video 4 mein Farrin ke naqshe ki ayaton ki ranges (screen par lagane se pehle asal paper se mila lein), aur Video 3 mein Imam Razi wala qaul (kitab ka naam screen par likhna ho to asal matn dekh lein). Dono fact-check sheet mein nishan-zad hain.

## Upload ki tarteeb

1 → 2 → 3 → 4. Chaaron ko ek playlist mein rakhiye; har video ki end screen par agli video lagaiye. Agar sirf ek se shuruaat karni ho to **Video 4** ya **Video 3** ka hook sab se tez hai (pehle 10 second mein dekhne wala khud check kar sakta hai).
