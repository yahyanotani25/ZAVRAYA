**In mashhoor daawon se bachiye (video mein jaan boojh kar shamil nahi kiye gaye):**

- **"Wasata lafzon ke hisaab se bhi surah ke bilkul beech mein hai."** — Nahi. Lafzon ki ginti ke hisaab se yeh taqreeban 41% par aata hai. Daawa sirf ayaton ki ginti ka hai — aur ring structure ka markaz kisi ginti par munhasir nahi.
- **"19 ka code", "Makkah golden ratio par hai", aur lafzon ke doosre jode (dunya/akhirat, farishte/shayateen waghera).** — "19" wala nazariya Rashad Khalifa se juda hai jis ne is ke liye ayaat tak ka inkaar kiya; golden ratio wala daawa naap-tol mein tik'ta nahi; aur lafzon ke baaqi jode is script ke liye check nahi kiye gaye — bina khud gine mat boliye. Is video ki har cheez woh hai jo dekhne wala khud mushaf khol kar check kar sakta hai.

**Comments mein yeh aitraz aayenge — tayyar jawab:**

- **"Ring structure to dekhne wale ki marzi hai, jahan chaho lakeer kheench do."** — Jaiz aitraz hai, aur kuch muhaqqiqeen (masalan Nicolai Sinai) yehi tanqeed karte hain. Is liye video mein woh misaalein li gayi hain jahan rabt lafzon mein hai, sirf mauzoo mein nahi: do naam ⟷ do naam, "illa" ⟷ "illa", "ibtala" ⟷ "la-nabluwannakum", "bima unzila ilayka" ⟷ "bima unzila ilayhi".
- **"Ayaton ke number to baad mein lage."** — Ayaton ke nishan (ru'us al-ay) Rasulullah ﷺ ki tilawat ke waqf se manqool hain; ginti ke mukhtalif mazhab unhi riwayaton par mabni hain. Aur ring ka markaz (qiblah wali ayaat) number ke baghair bhi wahin hai.
- **"Arabi shayari mein bhi to san'atein hoti hain."** — Bilkul; farq yeh hai ke shayar likh kar, kaat kar, mahinon mehnat kar ke ek qaseeda banata hai. Yahan 23 saal ka zabani, halaat ke jawab mein utarne wala kalaam hai.
