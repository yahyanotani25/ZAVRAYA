### [2:45] PART 2 — Quran: beej, paani... aur aag

Surah Al-Waqi'ah mein Allah teen sawal poochta hai. Teeno ka andaaz ek hi hai.

Pehla: "Jo beej tum bote ho — usay tum ugaate ho, ya ugaane wale Hum hain?"

Doosra: "Jo paani tum peete ho — baadal se tumne utara, ya Hum ne?"

"Bhala woh AAG dekho jo tum sulgaate ho: uska darakht tumne paida kiya — ya paida karne wale Hum hain?"

Aag. Wohi aag.

Namrood lakdiyon ka pahaad jalata hai. Sholay aasman se baatein kar rahe hain. Aur Hazrat Ibrahim (alayhis-salam) ko us mein phenk diya jata hai.

### [4:45] PART 3 — Ghadi wali kainat

1687. Newton harkat ke qawaneen deta hai — aur Europe jhoom uthta hai. Ab har cheez ka hisaab lagaya ja sakta tha — hazaar saal baad grahan kis din lagega, yeh bhi.

French scientist Laplace ne is soch ko aakhri had tak pohancha diya. Usne kaha: agar koi zabardast dimagh aaj kainat ke har zarre ki jagah aur raftaar jaan le — to woh poora mustaqbil bata sakta hai. Us ke liye kuch bhi ghair-yaqeeni nahi hoga.

Ek mashhoor waqia hai. Napoleon ne Laplace se poocha: "Tumne kainat par itni badi kitab likhi — us mein Khuda ka zikr kyun nahi?"

Laplace ka jawab: "Janab — mujhe us mafrooze ki zaroorat hi nahi padi."

Yeh thi "ghadi wali kainat." Ek dafa chaabi bhar do — phir khud chalti rahegi.

Is kainat mein aag HAMESHA jalayegi — kyunke jalana aag ke "andar" hai. Mo'jiza? Namumkin. Dua? Be-asar. Aur Ghazali? Purane zamane ka ek maulvi.

Do sau saal tak yehi science ka aqeedah raha.

Aur phir ek chhota sa experiment hua. Ek deewar. Do darzein. Aur ek aisa natija — jisne Laplace ki ghadi ke purze bikher diye.

### [8:20] PART 5 — Einstein ki haar

Einstein ko yeh baat sakht na-pasand thi. Usne likha: "Khuda paanse nahi phenkta."

Uska yaqeen tha: zaroor koi chhupi hui wajah hai — bas humein abhi maloom nahi.

Ek din sair karte hue Einstein achanak ruka aur apne saathi se poocha: "Kya tum waqai maante ho ke chaand sirf tab maujood hota hai — jab main usay dekhta hoon?"

Yeh behas 30 saal chalti rahi. Phir 1964 mein ek physicist — John Bell — ne ek tareeqa nikala jis se experiment kar ke faisla ho sakta tha: Einstein sahi hai — ya quantum physics?

Experiment hue. 1972 mein. 1982 mein. 2015 mein. Har baar pehle se zyada sakht.

Har baar ek hi natija:

Einstein ghalat tha.

Zarron ki khususiyaat naapne se pehle us tarah "tay-shuda" nahi hotin — jis tarah Einstein chahta tha. Isi kaam par 2022 ka Nobel Prize mila.

Laplace ki ghadi? Toot chuki hai.

Kainat ki bunyaad mein insaan ko "pakke sabab" nahi mile. "Imkanaat" mile.

### [9:20] PART 6 — Suraj ko jalna nahi chahiye tha

Unhein jodne ke liye itni zabardast takkar chahiye... ke purani physics ke hisaab se Suraj ka markaz jitna garam hai — us se sainkdon guna zyada garam hona chahiye tha.

Suraj itna garam nahi hai.

### [10:45] PART 7 — Woh lafz wapas aata hai

Ab zara ruk kar dekhiye — humne kya paya.

Purani science kehti thi: har cheez ke andar apni pakki taqat hai. Sabab apne natije ko majboor karta hai. Kainat khud chalti hai.

Nayi science ne kya dekha?

Bunyaadi satah par natija pakka nahi — sirf imkaan hai.

Ek hi haalat — do alag natije.

Aur jo "qawaneen" humein itne pakke lagte hain — aag jalati hai, patthar girta hai — woh kharbon zarron ka ausat hain. Ek ma'mool. Ek...

"aadat."

> **[TEXT]** "ʿĀDAT" — Ghazali, 1095 · "STATISTICAL REGULARITY" — Physics, 1927

Yeh lafz yaad hai?

Aur yeh sirf meri baat nahi. 1993 mein America ki ek chemistry professor — Karen Harding — ne ek research paper likha: "Causality Then and Now: Al-Ghazali and Quantum Theory." Us mein unhone dikhaya ke Ghazali ki baat aur quantum theory ki sab se mashhoor tashreeh mein hairat-angez mumasilat hai.

Ab phir insaaf ki baat.

Quantum physics Allah ko "sabit" nahi karti — yeh physics ka kaam hi nahi. Aur scientists mein aaj bhi behas hai ke in experiments ka gehra matlab kya hai.

Lekin ek cheez pakki hai. Woh daleel — ke "kainat ek band machine hai, is liye na Khuda ki zaroorat hai, na mo'jiza mumkin hai" — us daleel ko science ne khud dafna diya hai.

Physics yahan aa kar ruk jati hai. Woh kehti hai: "50 feesad chance." Woh yeh nahi bata sakti ke IS baar yehi kyun hua.

Einstein ne kaha tha: "Khuda paanse nahi phenkta." Kaha jata hai ke Niels Bohr ne jawab diya: "Einstein — Khuda ko mat batao ke usay kya karna hai."

Jo cheez hamare liye "chance" hai... woh uske liye ilm hai.

### [12:35] PART 8 — "Al-Qayyum"

**Aqeedah ka ek zaroori note (taake koi maslaki aitraz na ho):** Asbaab ke baare mein Ahl-e-Sunnat ke andar tafseeli behas hai. Asha'irah (jin mein Ghazali hain) kehte hain asbaab mein apni koi taseer nahi. Maturidiyyah aur Imam Ibn Taymiyyah jaise ulama kehte hain Allah ne asbaab mein taseer rakhi hai. Lekin **ek baat par sab muttafiq hain: koi sabab Allah ke hukm aur izn ke baghair asar nahi karta, aur Woh jab chahe us asar ko rok de.** Script isi muttafiqa baat par khatam hoti hai — is liye "aag mein apni taqat hai ya nahi" ki behas mein comments mein mat uljhiye.

**Comments mein yeh aitraz aayenge — tayyar jawab:**

- **"Quantum randomness se Khuda sabit nahi hota."** — Bilkul. Video yeh daawa karti hi nahi. Daawa sirf itna hai: "kainat ek band, pakki machine hai is liye mo'jiza namumkin hai" — yeh daleel ab science ke andar se toot chuki hai.
- **"Many-Worlds ya Bohmian mechanics mein to sab deterministic hai."** — Durust, yeh tashreehat maujood hain; kisi ek par ittefaq nahi. Lekin har tashreeh mein woh purani tasveer — chhote thos zarre jin ki pehle se tay-shuda local khususiyaat hon — khatam ho chuki hai. Nobel 2022 isi par mila.
- **"Agar aag jalati nahi to haath kyun jalta hai?"** — Script ka aakhri hissa yehi jawab deta hai: Allah ki sunnat mustaqil hai, is liye asbaab ikhtiyar karna farz hai. Farq sirf yeh hai ke bharosa kis par ho.
