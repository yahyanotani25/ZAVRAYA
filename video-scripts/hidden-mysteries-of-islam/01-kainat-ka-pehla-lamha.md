## 2. Script


### [0:00] COLD OPEN — Einstein ki ghalti

> **[VISUAL]** Kaali screen. Beech mein ek safed nuqta — dil ki tarah dhadakta hua. Typewriter text: "1917 — BERLIN".
> **[SFX]** Ghadi ki tik-tik, neeche halka sa drone.

Saal 1917. Berlin.

Duniya ka sab se bada dimagh — Albert Einstein — apni hi equations ko ghoor raha hai. Aur equations usay ek aisi baat bata rahi hain jo usay bilkul pasand nahi:

Kainat ruki hui NAHI hai.

To Einstein ne kya kiya? Apni hi equation mein ek extra number zabardasti daal diya — taake kainat ruki hui nazar aaye. Baad mein usne isay apni zindagi ki "sab se badi ghalti" kaha.

> **[VISUAL]** Equation mein ek laal "Λ" chamakta hai — phir us par cross lag jata hai.

Lekin asal kahani yeh nahi.

Asal kahani yeh hai ke Einstein se 1300 saal pehle — Arab ke registan mein — ek kitab mein do lafz maujood the. Aur un do lafzon ka seedha ma'ni wohi hai jo Einstein ki equations keh rahi thin... aur jise maan'ne se khud Einstein ne inkaar kar diya.

> **[TEXT]** وَإِنَّا لَمُوسِعُونَ — dheere dheere roshan hota hua.

Aaj: woh teen ayaat jo kainat ki poori kahani suna deti hain. Woh kabootar jinhone do scientists ko Nobel Prize dilwa diya. Aur aakhir mein Quran ka woh sawal — sirf do ayaton ka — jise sun kar Quraysh ke ek sardar ka beta keh utha: "Qareeb tha ke mera dil ud jaye."

Chaliye — waqt ko ulta chalate hain.

> **[OPTIONAL · 3 sec]** Channel ka logo sting. Agar salam karna ho to yahan — hook ke BAAD, pehle nahi: "Assalamu alaikum — yeh hai Hidden Mysteries of Islam."

### [1:10] PART 1 — Woh kainat jo "hamesha se thi"

Pehle yeh samjhiye ke Einstein itna pareshan kyun tha.

Aristotle ke zamane se — do hazaar saal se zyada — science aur falsafe ki ghalib raaye ek hi thi: kainat hamesha se hai. Na iski koi shuruaat, na yeh badalti hai. Sitare apni jagah latke hue hain — jaise deewar par tangi hui tasveer.

Aur sochiye: agar kainat hamesha se hai... to "banane wale" ka sawal hi khatam. Jo cheez kabhi shuru hi nahi hui — usay shuru kisne kiya?

Mashhoor falsafi Bertrand Russell ne BBC radio par yehi kaha tha: "Kainat bas hai — aur baat khatam."

Yeh tha inkaar ka sab se mazboot qila.

Phir Einstein ki apni theory — General Relativity — aati hai. Aur uski maths kehti hai: yeh tasveer deewar par tik hi nahi sakti. Kainat ya to phailegi — ya sikudegi. Ruk nahi sakti.

Jaise aap gend hawa mein uchhalein. Woh ya upar ja rahi hogi — ya neeche aa rahi hogi. Hawa mein ruki hui gend? Namumkin.

Einstein ko apni hi maths par yaqeen nahi aaya. Usne woh extra number jod diya — taake gend hawa mein ruki rahe.

Aur phir kahani mein ek aisa kirdaar aata hai jiski kisi ko tawaqqo nahi thi. Ek padri — jo physicist bhi tha. Georges Lemaître.

1927 mein Lemaître ne Einstein ki hi equations se dikhaya: kainat phail rahi hai. Aur agar aaj phail rahi hai... to kal chhoti thi. Parson aur chhoti. Film ko ulta chalate jaiye — to ek lamha aisa aayega jab sab kuch ek nuqte mein simta hua tha.

Einstein ka jawab? Lemaître ke apne bayan ke mutabiq: "Tumhara hisaab durust hai — lekin tumhari physics bardasht se bahar hai."

Sirf do saal baad. 1929. California ki ek pahadi par Edwin Hubble us waqt ki sab se badi telescope se door ki galaxies dekhta hai.

Door ki galaxies hum se door bhaag rahi hain. Aur jo jitni door hai — utni hi tez.

> **[VISUAL]** Galaxies har taraf door hat'ti hui; roshni surkh hoti hui (redshift).

Kainat phail rahi thi. Einstein ghalat tha. Padri sahi tha.

Aur ab... woh ayat suniye jo us waqt 1300 saal purani ho chuki thi.

### [3:15] PART 2 — Do lafz: "La-musi'un"

> **[TILAWAT · Adh-Dhariyat 51:47]**
>
> وَٱلسَّمَآءَ بَنَيْنَٰهَا بِأَيْي۟دٍۢ وَإِنَّا لَمُوسِعُونَ

"Aur aasman ko Hum ne quwwat se banaya — aur yaqeenan Hum wus'at dene wale hain."

Is aakhri lafz par rukiye. "La-musi'un."

Arabi mein kaam batane ke do tareeqe hain. Ek: "Ahmad ne gaadi chalayi." Kaam hua — khatam. Doosra: "Ahmad driver hai." Yeh guzra hua kaam nahi. Yeh uski pehchaan hai. Jaari sifat.

Quran ne yahan doosra tareeqa chuna. "Hum ne phailaya" nahi. "Hum phailane wale HAIN."

Ab insaaf ki baat — kyunke is channel par hum aap se sirf sach kahenge.

Purane mufassireen ne is lafz ke do ma'ni likhe hain. Ek: "Hum badi qudrat wale hain." Doosra — jo Imam Ibn Kathir ne likha — "Hum ne isay wasee' kar diya." Aur Maulana Maududi ne dono ma'ni likh kar kaha: doosre ma'ni ke mutabiq yeh kainat musalsal phail rahi hai.

To daawa yeh nahi ke saatwin sadi ke Arab "Big Bang" jaante the. Zahir hai nahi jaante the.

Sawal yeh hai: jis zamane mein poori duniya aasman ko ek thehri hui chhat samajhti thi — us zamane mein aasman ke liye yeh lafz kisne chuna?

Aur 1998 mein ek aur baat pata chali: yeh phailao ruk nahi raha. Tez ho raha hai.

Lekin rukiye. Yeh to sirf "phailao" hai. Asli jhatka tab lagta hai... jab aap film ko poora ulta chalate hain.

### [4:40] PART 3 — "Ratq" aur "Fatq"

Peeche chaliye. Ek arab saal... das arab saal... 13.8 arab saal peeche.

Wahan kya hai?

Na sitare. Na galaxies. Na zameen. Na aasman. Saara maada, saari energy — ek intehai garam, intehai ghani haalat mein. Yakja. Juda hua.

Ab Surah Al-Anbiya, ayat 30:

> **[TILAWAT · Al-Anbiya 21:30]**
>
> أَوَلَمْ يَرَ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُوٓا۟ أَنَّ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلْأَرْضَ كَانَتَا رَتْقًۭا فَفَتَقْنَٰهُمَا

"Kya inkaar karne walon ne nahi dekha ke aasman aur zameen aapas mein jude hue the — phir Hum ne unhein juda juda kar diya?"

Do lafz. "Ratq." Aur "Fatq."

> **[TEXT]** رَتْقًۭا ← ek sila hua kapda · فَفَتَقْنَٰهُمَا ← silai udhadti hui

"Ratq" — Arabi mein us cheez ko kehte hain jo si di gayi ho. Band. Judi hui. Beech mein koi darz nahi.

"Fatq" — us silai ko udhed dena. Cheer kar alag kar dena.

To ayat keh rahi hai: aasman aur zameen — yaani yeh poori kainat — ek sili hui, yakja cheez thi. Phir Hum ne usay udhed diya.

Aur yeh koi nayi "modern" tafsir nahi. Tabi'een ke imam — Hasan Basri aur Qatadah — ne 1300 saal pehle yehi kaha: "Dono jude hue the — phir Allah ne unhein juda kar diya."

Ab ayat ka pehla lafz dekhiye: "Awalam yara" — "kya unhone DEKHA nahi?"

Saatwin sadi mein kaun dekh sakta tha? Koi nahi.

Lekin 1964 mein... insaan ne waqai dekh liya.

Aur yahan kahani mein kabootar aate hain.

### [6:00] PART 4 — Kabootar aur kainat ki goonj

1964. America. New Jersey. Do radio astronomers — Arno Penzias aur Robert Wilson — ek bade, seeng jaise antenna par kaam kar rahe hain.

Aur antenna mein ek ajeeb shor hai. "Shhhhh."

Antenna jis taraf bhi ghumao — shor wahi. Din mein — wahi. Raat mein — wahi. Garmi, sardi — wahi.

Unhone socha: antenna kharab hai. Andar jhaank kar dekha — to kabootaron ne ghonsla bana rakha tha. Aur antenna... un ki beet se bhara pada tha.

Penzias ne badi sharafat se isay ek science-wala naam diya: "white dielectric material." Safed maada.

Do PhD scientists ne apne haathon se woh antenna saaf kiya. Kabootar hata diye gaye.

Shor... wahin ka wahin.

> **[SFX]** TV static "shhhh" — do second — phir achanak khamoshi.

Kyunke woh shor antenna ka nahi tha.

Woh shor kainat ka tha.

Woh us pehle lamhe ki bachi hui roshni thi — jo 13.8 arab saal se safar kar rahi hai, aur aaj bhi aasman ke har kone se aa rahi hai.

Aapko yaad hai — purane antenna wale TV par jab channel nahi lagta tha to screen par "jhilmil" aati thi? Us jhilmil ka ek chhota sa hissa... yehi roshni thi.

Aap apne drawing room mein baith kar kainat ki paidaish ki goonj dekh rahe the. Aur aapko pata bhi nahi tha.

1978 mein dono ko Nobel Prize mila.

"Kya unhone nahi dekha?"

Dekh liya.

### [7:20] PART 5 — "Dhuaan" aur paani

> **[✂️ OPTIONAL — yahan se]** Chhota version chahiye to yeh hissa nikaal dein; kahani nahi toot'ti.

Ek aur ayat. Aur yahan chemistry aati hai.

> **[TILAWAT · Fussilat 41:11]**
>
> ثُمَّ ٱسْتَوَىٰٓ إِلَى ٱلسَّمَآءِ وَهِىَ دُخَانٌۭ

"Phir Woh aasman ki taraf mutawajjeh hua — aur woh us waqt dhuaan tha."

Dhuaan — yeh lafz kyun?

Shuru ki kainat mein na pathar tha, na paani, na loha. Sirf do halki gasein: taqreeban 75 feesad hydrogen, 25 feesad helium. Itni garam aur ghani — ke roshni bhi us mein se seedhi guzar nahi sakti thi. Ek garam, dhundla, gas ka samandar.

Aur usi ayat 21:30 ka agla tukda:

> **[TILAWAT · Al-Anbiya 21:30 — agla hissa]**
>
> وَجَعَلْنَا مِنَ ٱلْمَآءِ كُلَّ شَىْءٍ حَىٍّ

"Aur Hum ne har zinda cheez paani se banayi."

Ek hi ayat. Pehle hisse mein kainat ki paidaish. Doosre mein zindagi ki bunyaad.

Aaj NASA jab doosre sayyaron par zindagi dhoondta hai — to uska usool kya hai? "Follow the water." Paani ka peecha karo. Kyunke aaj tak ek bhi aisa jaandaar nahi mila jo paani ke baghair jee sake.

> **[✂️ OPTIONAL — yahan tak]**

### [8:20] PART 6 — Woh sawal jis ne dil uda diya

Ab yahan ek samajhdar aadmi kahega:

"Theek hai, kainat ki shuruaat hui. Lekin is se Khuda kaise sabit hua? Ho sakta hai yeh sab khud ba khud ho gaya ho."

Bohat acha sawal hai. Aur kamaal yeh hai ke yeh sawal... Quran ne khud uthaya hai. Sirf do ayaton mein.

Ab woh waqia — jiska wada maine shuru mein kiya tha.

Jang-e-Badr ke baad ka zamana hai. Quraysh ke sardar Mut'im bin Adi ka beta — Jubayr — Madinah aaya hai. Apne qaidiyon ko chhudwane. Woh abhi Musalman nahi.

Maghrib ka waqt hai. Rasulullah ﷺ namaz mein Surah At-Tur ki tilawat farma rahe hain. Jubayr sun raha hai. Aur phir yeh ayaat aati hain:

> **[TILAWAT · At-Tur 52:35-36]**
>
> أَمْ خُلِقُوا۟ مِنْ غَيْرِ شَىْءٍ أَمْ هُمُ ٱلْخَٰلِقُونَ
>
> أَمْ خَلَقُوا۟ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلْأَرْضَ ۚ بَل لَّا يُوقِنُونَ

"Kya yeh kisi cheez ke baghair hi paida ho gaye? Ya yeh khud apne khaliq hain? Ya inhone aasmanon aur zameen ko paida kiya hai? Nahi — balke yeh yaqeen hi nahi rakhte."

Jubayr kehte hain — aur yeh Sahih Bukhari ki riwayat hai: "Kaada qalbi an yatir." — "Qareeb tha ke mera dil ud jaye."

Kyun? In do ayaton mein aisa kya hai?

Dhyan se suniye — yeh is video ka sab se aham hissa hai.

Jo cheez pehle nahi thi — aur phir wujood mein aa gayi — uske sirf chaar hi raaste ho sakte hain. Paanchwan koi nahi.

> **[VISUAL]** Chaar darwaze. Har ek par number. Ek ek kar ke band honge.

Pehla raasta: woh "kuch nahi" se — bina kisi sabab ke — khud ba khud aa gayi.

Aap raat ko kamra band kar ke soye. Subah utthe — to kamre ke beech mein ek haathi khada hai. Aap poochte hain: yeh kahan se aaya? Jawab milta hai: "Kahin se nahi. Bas aa gaya."

Aap maan lenge?

"Kuch nahi" ke paas na taqat hai, na qanoon, na imkaan. Zero jama zero jama zero... zero hi rehta hai.

Aur jab koi scientist kehta hai "kainat nothing se bani" — to uska "nothing" asal mein energy, fields aur physics ke qawaneen se bhara hota hai. Woh "kuch nahi" nahi hai. Sawal wahin khada hai: woh qawaneen kahan se aaye?

> **[SFX]** Pehla darwaza dhadaam se band.

Doosra raasta: usne khud ko khud bana liya. Yaani banne se pehle hi maujood tha. Yeh aisa hai jaise koi kahe: "Meri ammi ne khud apne aap ko janam diya." Namumkin.

> **[SFX]** Doosra darwaza band.

Teesra raasta: usay kisi aur bani hui cheez ne banaya. Aur usay kisi aur ne. Aur usay kisi aur ne... kabhi na khatam hone wala silsila.

Ek misaal suniye.

Mujhe 100 rupay chahiyen. Main aap se maangta hoon. Aap kehte hain: "Mere paas nahi — apne dost se le kar deta hoon." Dost kehta hai: "Mere paas bhi nahi — main apne dost se leta hoon." Aur woh apne dost se. Aur woh apne dost se...

Agar yeh silsila kabhi khatam hi na ho — agar kisi ke paas bhi apna paisa na ho — to kya mere haath mein kabhi 100 ka note aayega?

Kabhi nahi.

Lekin note mere haath mein hai. Kainat maujood hai. Aap maujood hain.

> **[SFX]** Teesra darwaza band. Sirf chautha khula — us mein se roshni.

Iska matlab: is silsile ke peeche koi ek aisa zaroor hai — jisne kisi se udhaar nahi liya. Jiska wujood uska apna hai. Jo kisi ka mohtaj nahi — aur sab jiske mohtaj hain.

Yehi chautha raasta hai. Aur yehi akela raasta bachta hai.

Ibn Sina ne isay kaha "Wajib-ul-Wujood." Aur Quran ne sirf do lafzon mein keh diya:

> **[TILAWAT · Al-Ikhlas 112:2]**
>
> ٱللَّهُ ٱلصَّمَدُ

"Allah be-niyaz hai."

Ab samjhe — Jubayr ka dil kyun udne laga tha? Do ayaton ne har raasta band kar diya tha.

Aur aaj West ki universities mein isi daleel ka naam kya hai? "Kalam Cosmological Argument." "Kalam" — yeh lafz Arabi ka hai. Musalman mutakallimeen se liya gaya.

### [12:10] PART 7 — Science aaj kahan khadi hai

Aur science aaj kahan khadi hai?

2003 mein teen cosmologists — Borde, Guth aur Vilenkin — ne ek theorem sabit kiya: jo kainat ausatan phailti rahi ho — woh maazi mein hamesha se nahi ho sakti. Uske maazi ki ek had hai.

Vilenkin ne likha: "Cosmologists ab ek hamesha-se-maujood kainat ke peeche nahi chhup sakte. Bhaagne ka koi raasta nahi."

Aur Stephen Hawking ne likha: "Taqreeban har koi ab yeh maanta hai ke kainat — aur khud waqt — ki ibtida Big Bang se hui."

Haan — us ibtida se "pehle" kya tha, is par physics mein behas jaari hai. Lekin "kainat hamesha se aisi hi thi" — woh purana qila gir chuka hai.

Aur NASA ke astronomer Robert Jastrow — jo khud ko agnostic kehte the — unhone jo likha, woh sunne se ta'alluq rakhta hai:

> **[VISUAL]** Ek tanha kohpaima barfeeli choti par aakhri chattan pakad kar upar aata hai.

"Scientist jahalat ke pahaad chadhta gaya. Sab se oonchi choti sar karne hi wala tha. Aur jab woh aakhri chattan par chadh kar upar aaya — to wahan mazhabi aalimon ka ek giroh us ka istiqbal kar raha tha... jo sadiyon se wahin baitha tha."

### [13:15] PART 8 — Anjaam, aur ek wada

Aur jis kitab ne ibtida batayi — usne anjaam bhi bataya hai:

> **[TILAWAT · Al-Anbiya 21:104]**
>
> يَوْمَ نَطْوِى ٱلسَّمَآءَ كَطَىِّ ٱلسِّجِلِّ لِلْكُتُبِ ۚ كَمَا بَدَأْنَآ أَوَّلَ خَلْقٍۢ نُّعِيدُهُۥ

"Jis din Hum aasman ko yun lapet denge jaise likhe hue kaghzaat ka tumaar lapet diya jata hai. Jaise Hum ne pehli takhleeq ki ibtida ki thi — waise hi usay lautayenge."

Jo khola gaya tha — woh lapet diya jayega.

Science abhi nahi jaanti ke kainat ka anjaam kya hoga — is liye hum yahan koi daawa nahi karte. Sirf itna: jis kitab ki ibtida wali baat sach nikli... uski anjaam wali baat ko halka mat lijiye.

To aaj humne kya dekha?

Einstein ne kaha: kainat ruki hui hai. Quran ne kaha: "Hum wus'at dene wale hain."

Falsafe ne kaha: kainat hamesha se hai. Quran ne kaha: "Yeh judi hui thi — Hum ne isay khola."

Aur jab insaan ne kaha: "Yeh sab khud ba khud ho gaya" — to Quran ne poocha: "Kya yeh kisi cheez ke baghair paida ho gaye — ya yeh khud khaliq hain?"

Quran physics ki kitab nahi. Yeh hidayat ki kitab hai. Lekin isne ek wada kiya tha:

> **[TILAWAT · Fussilat 41:53]**
>
> سَنُرِيهِمْ ءَايَٰتِنَا فِى ٱلْءَافَاقِ وَفِىٓ أَنفُسِهِمْ حَتَّىٰ يَتَبَيَّنَ لَهُمْ أَنَّهُ ٱلْحَقُّ

"Hum unhein apni nishaniyan dikhayenge — aafaaq mein bhi, aur khud unki apni jaanon mein bhi — yahan tak ke un par khul jayega ke yehi haq hai."

Aafaaq mein — door aasmanon mein — aaj humne dekh liya.

Lekin "khud unki jaanon mein"? Aapke apne andar?

Agli video mein hum kainat ki sab se chhoti duniya mein utrenge — atom ke andar. Jahan ek cheez ek hi waqt mein do jagah hoti hai. Aur wahan aapko Imam Ghazali ka woh jumla milega — jis par duniya 900 saal hansti rahi:

"Aag kapde ko nahi jalati."

Woh video screen par aa chuki hai. Wahin milte hain.

**Comments mein yeh aitraz aayenge — tayyar jawab:**

- **"21:30 mein zameen ka zikr hai, aur zameen to Big Bang ke arbon saal baad bani."** — Quran mein "as-samawat wal-ard" (aasman aur zameen) poori kainat ke liye muhavra hai, jaise hum kehte hain "zameen-aasman ek kar diye". Ayat ka daawa: jo aaj alag alag hai woh kabhi yakja tha.
- **"BGV theorem se Khuda sabit nahi hota; Alan Guth khud nahi maante."** — Durust. Guth ke nazdeek ibtida lazmi sabit nahi, aur Sean Carroll kehte hain ke quantum gravity mein theorem laagu na ho. - **"41:9-12 mein zameen pehle aur aasman baad mein hai."** — "Thumma" Arabi mein hamesha waqt ki tarteeb nahi batata; bayan ki tarteeb ke liye bhi aata hai. 79:27-30 mein aasman ka zikr pehle hai.
