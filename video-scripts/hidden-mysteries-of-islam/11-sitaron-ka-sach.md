### [1:20] PART 1 — Latoo

Pehle yeh samjhiye ke "star sign" hota kya hai.

Saal bhar mein Suraj aasman par sitaron ke barah jhurmuton ke saamne se guzarta hai. Inhein burj kehte hain. Jis din aap paida hue — Suraj jis burj ke saamne tha — woh aap ka "star."

Yeh hisaab Babylon ke logon ne baandha tha. Do hazaar saal se zyada pehle.

Ab ek latoo ghumaiye.

> **[VISUAL]** Zameen par tezi se ghoomta hua latoo. Us ka oopar wala sira dheere dheere ek daira bana raha hai.

Latoo ghoomta hai — lekin us ka sar bhi dheere dheere dolta hai. Ek chhota sa daira banata hua.

Zameen bhi ek latoo hai. Us ka mehwar bhi dol raha hai. Bohat dheere.

> **[FORMULA]** Ek poora daira = 25,772 saal → 1 degree = 71.6 saal

Ek poora daira: taqreeban 26 hazaar saal mein. Yaani har 71.6 saal mein aasman ek degree khisak jata hai.

Ab calculator. Babylon ke hisaab ko guzre: taqreeban 2,100 saal.

> **[TEXT — calculator]** 2,100 ÷ 71.6 = 29.3 DEGREE

2,100 taqseem 71.6. Jawab: 29.3 degree.

Aur ek burj kitna chauda hota hai? 30 degree.

*(pause)*

Aasman poora ek burj khisak chuka hai. Akhbar wahin khada hai.

> **[TEXT — do columns]** AKHBAR: Hamal (Aries) 21 March – 19 April ‖ AASMAN: Suraj Hoot (Pisces) mein 12 March – 18 April

Misaal. Aap 25 March ko paida hue. Akhbar kehta hai: aap ka star Hamal hai — Aries. Aasman kehta hai: us din Suraj Hoot mein tha — Pisces.

Aur is se bhi ajeeb. 29 November se 17 December tak Suraj ek aise burj ke saamne hota hai... jo aap ke akhbar mein hai hi nahi. Us ka naam hai Ophiuchus. Terahwan burj.

Aur Aqrab — Scorpio — wale suniye: Suraj aap ke burj mein saal mein sirf chhe-saat din rehta hai.

### [2:55] PART 2 — Doctor banaam Mars

> **[TEXT — do palde]** MARS: zameen ke das-wen hisse jitna bhaari — lekin 5.6 crore kilometer door ‖ DOCTOR: sirf 70 kilo — lekin 30 centimeter door

Mars — jab zameen ke sab se qareeb ho — tab bhi saadhe paanch crore kilometer door hota hai.

> **[TEXT — calculator]** MARS: 0.000000048 newton · DOCTOR: 0.00000018 newton → DOCTOR ≈ 4 GUNA

### [3:50] PART 3 — 28 najoomi, ek imtihaan

"Chalo — wajah samajh nahi aati. Lekin kaam to karta hai."

1985. Duniya ke sab se mo'tabar science journals mein se ek — Nature. Ek naujawan physicist — Shawn Carlson — 28 maane hue najoomiyon ko bulata hai. Imtihaan ka tareeqa najoomiyon ki apni marzi se tay hota hai.

Har najoomi ko ek shakhs ka zaicha diya jata hai — aur teen shakhsiyaton ke khaake. Un mein se ek us shakhs ka asli khaaka hai. Bas itna batana hai: kaunsa?

Najoomiyon ne khud kaha: hum kam az kam aadhe sahi batayenge.

Natija?

"To phir mera horoscope itna sahi kyun lagta hai?"

Yeh suniye. Aur imaandari se batayiye ke yeh aap ke baare mein kitna sach hai.

### [5:25] PART 4 — Madinah ka grahan

Ab wapas chaliye. Madinah. Grahan ka din.

Ek baap ne abhi abhi apna atharah maheene ka beta dafnaya hai. Aasman par Suraj ghat raha hai. Aur log keh rahe hain: yeh Ibrahim ki wafat ki wajah se hai.

Sochiye — agar koi shakhs jhoota daawa kar raha ho. Us ke liye yeh aasman se utra hua tohfa tha. Kuch kehna bhi nahi. Sirf khamosh rehna hai.

"Suraj aur chaand Allah ki nishaniyon mein se do nishaniyan hain. Inhein na kisi ki maut ki wajah se grahan lagta hai — na kisi ki zindagi ki wajah se."

### [7:50] PART 5 — To sitare kis liye hain?

To phir sitare hain kis liye?

Quran ne teen kaam bataye hain. Tabi'een ke imam Qatadah ne — Sahih Bukhari mein — inhein gin kar bataya, aur kaha: jo is ke siwa kuch aur dhoonde — us ne ghalti ki.

> **[TILAWAT · Al-Mulk 67:5]**
>
> وَلَقَدْ زَيَّنَّا ٱلسَّمَآءَ ٱلدُّنْيَا بِمَصَٰبِيحَ وَجَعَلْنَٰهَا رُجُومًۭا لِّلشَّيَٰطِينِ

Ek: aasman ki zeenat. Do: shayateen ke liye maar.

Aur teesra kaam:

> **[TILAWAT · An-Nahl 16:16]**
>
> وَبِٱلنَّجْمِ هُمْ يَهْتَدُونَ

"Aur sitaron se woh raasta paate hain."

Raasta. Qismat nahi — raasta.

Aur Musalmanon ne is hukm ko itni sanjeedgi se liya ke aaj aasman un ke naamon se bhara hua hai.

> **[TEXT — ek ek sitara chamakta hai, saath Arabi asal]** ALDEBARAN — الدبران · BETELGEUSE — يد الجوزاء · RIGEL — رجل · VEGA — النسر الواقع · ALTAIR — النسر الطائر · DENEB — ذنب

Aldebaran. Betelgeuse. Rigel. Vega. Altair. Deneb. Yeh sab Arabi naam hain. Aaj jin sitaron ke naam hain — un mein se do sau se zyada ke naam Arabi se aaye hain.

Aur ab us pahadi par chaliye.

Zila Jhelum. Pind Dadan Khan ke qareeb. Nandana ka qila. Aaj se taqreeban hazaar saal pehle.

> **[VISUAL]** Koh-e-Namak ki ek pahadi ki choti. Ek shakhs haath mein peetal ka aala liye door ufuq ko dekh raha hai.

Ek Musalman scientist — Al-Biruni — ek pahadi par chadhta hai. Us ke paas na satellite hai, na jahaz. Sirf ek aala — aur ek khayal.

Us ne do cheezein naapin. Pahadi ki oonchai. Aur choti se ufuq kitna neeche jhuka hua nazar aata hai — aadhe degree se zara zyada.

> **[FORMULA]** R = (h × cos θ) ÷ (1 − cos θ)

h — pahadi ki oonchai. θ — ufuq ka jhukao. R — zameen ka radius.

Us ka jawab: taqreeban 6,336 kilometer.

Aaj satellite se naapi hui qeemat? 6,371.

Farq: aadha feesad.

Pakistan ki ek pahadi se. Hazaar saal pehle. Poori zameen.

Yeh farq hai. Najoomi sitaron se poochhta raha: "Mera kal kaisa hoga?" Aur Musalman ne sitaron se poocha: "Main kahan khada hoon — aur yeh sab kis ne banaya?"

> **[TILAWAT · Aal-e-Imran 3:191]**
>
> وَيَتَفَكَّرُونَ فِى خَلْقِ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلْأَرْضِ رَبَّنَا مَا خَلَقْتَ هَٰذَا بَٰطِلًۭا سُبْحَٰنَكَ

"Woh aasmanon aur zameen ki takhleeq mein ghaur karte hain — aur kehte hain: ae hamare Rab! Tu ne yeh sab be-maqsad nahi banaya. Tu paak hai."

### [9:50] PART 6 — Aaj raat ka mission

Sitare aap ki qismat nahi likhte. Aap ki qismat us ke haath mein hai — jis ne sitare banaye.

Lekin sitare aaj bhi ek kaam kar sakte hain. Wohi jo Quran ne bataya: raasta dikhana.

> **[TEXT]** AAJ RAAT KA MISSION 🌙

Aaj raat shumal ki taraf munh kar ke khade hon. Saat sitaron ka woh jhurmut dhoondiye jo ek bade chamche jaisa hai. Chamche ke kinare ke do sitaron ki seedh mein aage badhiye — ek akela, madham sa sitara milega.

Qutbi sitara.

Ab apna baazu seedha kijiye aur mutthi band kijiye. Ek mutthi — taqreeban das degree. Ginte jaiye ke ufuq se us sitare tak kitni mutthiyan aati hain.

> **[FORMULA]** Qutbi sitare ki oonchai (degree) = aap ka arz-ul-balad (latitude)

Karachi: dhai mutthi. Lahore: teen. Islamabad: saadhe teen.

Jitni mutthiyan — utna aap ka latitude. Aap ne abhi — apne haath se — zameen par apni jagah naap li.

"Wa bin-najmi hum yahtadun."

Lekin kuch sitare aise bhi hain jo raasta nahi dikhate. Woh chhup jate hain. Aur jo cheez un ke qareeb aaye — usay roshni samet... nigal jate hain.

Un ka raaz Lahore mein paida hone wale ek unnees saal ke ladke ne khola tha. Aur us din Einstein — ek baar phir — ghalat sabit hua.
