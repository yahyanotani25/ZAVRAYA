### [1:20] PART 1 — Chhe atom

Kainat ka anjaam do taqaton ki kushti hai.

Ek taqat: phailao. Jo har cheez ko door le ja raha hai.

Doosri: gravity. Jo har cheez ko wapas kheench rahi hai.

Kaun jeetega? Is ka faisla ek number karta hai.

> **[FORMULA]** ρ = 3 × H² ÷ ( 8 × π × G )

Isay kehte hain "faisla-kun kasaafat." H — kainat ke phailne ki raftaar. G — gravity ka number.

Matlab ek jumle mein: agar kainat mein maada is number se ZYADA hai — to gravity jeetegi. Kainat ruk kar wapas giregi. Agar KAM hai — to phailao jeetega. Hamesha ke liye.

Aur yeh number kitna hai?

> **[TEXT]** 1 CUBIC METER — 6 ATOM

Fi cubic meter — taqreeban chhe atom.

Aap ke kamre ki hawa ke har cubic meter mein ek ke aage pachees sifar jitne atom hain. Aur kainat ka anjaam is par khada hai ke khalaa mein ausatan chhe hain... ya paanch.

To scientists ne gina. Saare sitare. Saari gas. Aur woh an-dekha maada bhi jo sirf apni kheench se pehchana jata hai.

Kul mila kar: zaroorat ka sirf ek tihai.

To faisla ho gaya? Phailao jeet gaya?

1998 tak sab yehi samajhte the. Phir pata chala: phailao sirf jaari nahi — TEZ ho raha hai. Khaali khalaa mein ek energy hai jo kainat ko dhakel rahi hai. Dark energy. Wohi — jis ka "120 sifar" wala number is channel ki "Mizan" wali video mein hai.

Aur ab poora anjaam ek hi sawal par tika hai: yeh energy aage kya karegi?

### [2:45] PART 2 — Pehla anjaam: jam jana

Agar yeh energy hamesha itni hi rahi — to anjaam hai: Badi Thand.

Vienna ke ek qabristan mein ek qabr hai. Us ke kutbe par — naam ke oopar — ek formula khuda hua hai.

> **[FORMULA]** S = k × log W

> **[VISUAL]** Ludwig Boltzmann ki qabr ka asli kutba — pathar par khuda hua formula.

Ludwig Boltzmann ka formula. "S" ka matlab hai be-tarteebi. Aur is formule ka sab se mashhoor natija ek saada sa jumla hai: be-tarteebi hamesha badhti hai.

Garam chai thandi ho jati hai. Thandi chai kabhi khud ba khud garam nahi hoti.

Kainat ek garam chai ka cup hai.

Sitare apna eindhan jala rahe hain. Naye sitare banane wali gas khatam ho rahi hai. Hisaab: ek ke aage chaudah sifar saal baad — aakhri sitara bhi bujh jayega.

> **[VISUAL]** Aasman ke sitare ek ek kar ke bujhte hain. Aakhri — ek madham surkh nuqta — bhi.

Phir sirf black holes bachenge. Aur ek ke aage SAU sifar saal baad — woh bhi bhaap ban kar ud chuke honge.

Aur phir? Kuch nahi. Thandi, andheri, khaali kainat. Hamesha.

### [5:10] PART 4 — Chautha anjaam: lapet diya jana

Aur ab chautha naqsha. Wapsi.

Agar dark energy kamzor pad jaye — to gravity ko phir mauqa mil jata hai. Phailao dheema hota hai. Rukta hai. Aur phir... kainat wapas sikudne lagti hai. Galaxies qareeb aati jati hain. Aasman garam hota jata hai. Aur aakhir mein sab kuch — wahin, jahan se chala tha.

> **[VISUAL]** Pehli video wali "phailti hui kainat" — ulti chalti hui.

Aur ek ajeeb baat. Is silsile ka pehla sawal yaad hai — raat ko aasman kaala kyun hai?

Pachees saal tak yeh naqsha almari mein band raha. Kyunke sab maante the: dark energy badalti nahi.

Phir America ki riyasat Arizona mein ek pahadi par — ek doorbeen mein paanch hazaar robotic aankhein lagayi gayin. Naam: DESI. Us ne taqreeban dedh crore galaxies ka teen-rukha naqsha banaya. Insaani tareekh ka sab se bada.

2024 mein pehla natija aaya. March 2025 mein doosra — aur zyada mazboot.

Ishara yeh tha: dark energy thehri hui nahi lagti. Woh... kamzor ho rahi hai.

Insaaf ki baat — aur yeh bohat zaroori hai. Yeh abhi "sabit" nahi hua. Scientists ke paimane par yeh ek mazboot ishara hai — saboot nahi. Aane wale chand saalon mein faisla hoga.

Lekin 2025 mein America ki Cornell University ke ek physicist ne hisaab laga kar dikhaya: agar yeh ishara sach nikla — to kainat ki kul umar taqreeban tentees arab saal hai. Chaudah guzar chuke hain. Gyarah arab saal baad phailao ruk jayega. Aur us ke baad —

**Comments mein yeh aitraz aayenge — tayyar jawab:**

- **"To Qayamat 20 arab saal baad hai?"** — Hargiz nahi. Script do baar kehti hai ke Qayamat ka waqt aur kaifiyat sirf Allah jaanta hai (7:187). Science sirf yeh bata rahi hai ke kainat hamesha nahi rahegi — kis tarah, yeh abhi us ke liye bhi khula sawal hai.
