**Comments mein yeh aitraz aayenge — tayyar jawab:**

- **"Saare bhaari anasir sitaron mein bane — sirf loha kyun?"** — Bilkul; carbon, oxygen, sona sab sitaron se aaye. Lekin loha khaas hai: yeh seedhi ka aakhri danda hai, yehi sitare ki maut ka sabab banta hai, aur Quran ne isi ke naam par surah rakhi aur isi ke liye "utaara" kaha.
- **"Anzala to maweshiyon aur libaas ke liye bhi aaya hai."** — Ji, aur script yeh khud batati hai. Daawa yeh nahi ke lafz ka sirf ek ma'ni hai; daawa yeh hai ke lafz ka seedha ma'ni — "utaarna" — lohe ke baare mein hairat-angez taur par durust nikla.
- **"Abjad wala hisaab marzi ka khel hai."** — Is liye yeh hissa ✂️ optional hai aur script mein saaf kaha gaya hai ke yeh saboot nahi. Hisaab har koi khud kar sakta hai; nateeja nikaalna dekhne wale par chhoda gaya hai.
