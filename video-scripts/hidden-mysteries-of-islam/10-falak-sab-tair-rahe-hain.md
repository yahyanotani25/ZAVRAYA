### [2:45] PART 2 — Jab science ne kaha: Suraj khada hai

Ab ek dilchasp baat. Yeh jumla — "sab tair rahe hain" — sadiyon tak science ke khilaf raha.

Phir 1783. William Herschel — wohi jis ne Uranus dhoonda tha — sitaron ki harkat ka hisaab lagata hai. Aur natija: Suraj khud bhi chal raha hai. Aur beeswin sadi mein pata chala: poori galaxy ghoom rahi hai.

Lekin lafz dekhiye. "Kullun fi falak" — har ek ka apna "falak." Aur Hazrat Ibn Abbas (raziyallahu anhu) ne "falak" ka matlab bataya: charkhe ki ghirni ki tarah — gol ghoomna.

Suraj ke liye. Chaand ke liye. Raat aur din ke liye. Sab ke liye.

Calculator nikaliye. Mars Suraj se zameen ke muqable mein 1.52 guna door hai.

> **[TEXT — calculator]** 1.52 × 1.52 × 1.52 = 3.51 → √3.51 = 1.87

1.52 ko teen baar zarb dijiye: 3.51. Ab is ka square root lijiye.

1.87.

> **[TEXT — calculator]** 5.2 × 5.2 × 5.2 = 140.6 → √140.6 = 11.86

Jawab: 11.86 saal. Asal naap? 11.86.

Ek formula. Poora nizam-e-shamsi.

- **"Quran to kehta hai Suraj zameen ke gird ghoomta hai."** — Quran ne kahin nahi kaha ke zameen ruki hui hai ya Suraj us ke gird ghoomta hai. Us ne kaha: Suraj chal raha hai, aur har ek apne falak mein hai — aur yeh dono baatein durust hain.
- **"Raat aur din bhi 'falak' mein kaise?"** — 21:33 mein raat, din, Suraj aur chaand ke baad "kullun" (sab) aaya hai. Bohat se mufassireen ke nazdeek muraad Suraj-chaand (aur sitare) hain; kuch isay zameen ki gardish ki taraf ishara samajhte hain.
- **"5 arab saal baad Qayamat?"** — Hargiz nahi. Script ne saaf kaha hai ke Qayamat ka waqt Allah ke siwa koi nahi jaanta (7:187); yeh sirf Suraj ke eindhan ka hisaab hai.
