# Kitni Der Thehre? — full video edit (Hidden Mysteries of Islam, Video 14)

Edit of the script in `video-scripts/hidden-mysteries-of-islam/14-kitni-der-thehre.md`, built around the channel
owner's own voice-over recording. 1920×1080, 30 fps, 12 min 18 s.

The finished MP4, the raw voice recording, the stock footage and the music files are **not** in this repo (size, and the
recording is private). This folder holds everything needed to rebuild the edit from those inputs, plus the credits.
The film engine (`film/src/engine.js`, `ui.js`, `kit3d.js`, `film.css`) is the one written for Video 1; everything
specific to this film is in `film/src/lib14.js` and `film/src/scenes/`.

## What was done

**Voice-over**
- The 37 min 44 s recording (many retakes) was split into 577 phrases by silence, every phrase transcribed on its own
  and aligned to the script. For every passage the **latest take** is used; earlier takes and abandoned starts are
  dropped. Result: 186 clips, 12 min 4 s. The finished track was transcribed again and read end to end: no repeated
  passage, no missing line.
- The speaker's own wording is kept where it differs from the script — above all the spoken ayah references that are
  not in the script ("Surah Al-Baqarah ki ayat number 259, jis ka tarjuma hai…", also before 18:25, 22:47, 23:99,
  97:3 and Surah Al-Asr). The on-screen text and the subtitles follow what was actually said (`edit/spoken.py`).
- One sentence is cut: script line "Dimagh waqt ko naapta nahi" was read as "…naapta **hai**" (the opposite meaning)
  and never retaken. It is removed at a clean pause; the next line ("Dimagh waqt ko… banata hai") carries the point.
- Pauses follow the script's punctuation, plus ten scripted long pauses (`EXTRA` in `edit/assemble.py`): the title
  sting, the 309.2 reveal and the three seconds of silence after the bet's question ("Bina dekhe bataiye: yeh video
  shuru hue kitni der hui?").
- Enhancement (`edit/ai_sr.py` + `edit/enhance2.py`): the phone recording has almost nothing above 5 kHz, so the top
  octaves are rebuilt with a neural bandwidth-extension model (ClearerVoice MossFormer2_SR_48K), then a linear-phase
  match-EQ, de-esser, gentle two-stage compression, soft saturation and a limiter; −16 LUFS.
  The file `Kitni_Der_Thehre_awaaz_sirf_retakes_hataye.wav` (retakes removed, otherwise untouched) was also given to
  the channel owner: an externally enhanced version of it (e.g. Adobe Podcast) is the same length and drops straight
  into `audio/mix.py` as `vo/final.wav`.

**Picture** — 77 scenes in 10 parts (`film/src/scenes/a.js … j.js`) plus one global overlay, every one timed to the
spoken words through `film/cues.json` (`L('<line>', '<word>')` looks a word up in the spoken text, so no timing is
typed by hand):
- **Teaser (first 70 s), cut like a film trailer**: 2.2:1 letterbox, a 3D silver coin ("300 SAAL PURANA" / "KAL"),
  "WOH JHOOT NAHI BOL RAHA.", the question in Arabic, the three-times table, three slams, "AAP BHI", date stamp
  "2010 · BOULDER, COLORADO" with the two clocks one foot apart, the three promises of the video, the bet ("ghadi mat
  dekhiye") with a pointer at the unlabelled counter, then the title over a night-sky time-lapse.
- **The unlabelled counter** in the bottom-left corner runs through the whole film at 70 per minute. In the last part
  it flies to the centre and is revealed as heartbeats — the pay-off of the bet.
- **Three questions = three chapters.** (1) 2:259: point-of-view walk through a ruined town, a hundred years of sky
  wheeling overhead, question and answer, the food that did not age and the three clocks; Newton against Einstein, the
  1971 flight ("MR. CLOCK" boarding passes, −59 / +273 ns), head and feet, the Earth's core, the muon through the palm
  of your hand, GPS, ayah 22:47. (2) 18:19: the cave with the sky turning outside, the coin in the bazaar, 18:25 and
  the calculator: 300 × 365.24 ÷ 354.37 = 309.2; then why the mind cannot measure time (operating table, film reel,
  the shrinking-years bar, the hadith). (3) 23:112–114: the question that has not been asked yet — the past tense
  "Qalu", the flat-line, the white plain, the answers shrinking from ten days to an evening.
- **The sum**: 70 ÷ 1000 × 24 = 1.68 → "1 GHANTA 41 MINUTE" on a 24-hour ring, 50,000 years → 2 minutes, the exam
  paper, then the good news — Laylat-ul-Qadr on the scales, Surah Al-Asr, and the answer to the bet: a live timer of
  the video against the viewer's guess, the heartbeat counter, "AAP KHUD WAQT HAIN."
- 3D (three.js): the coin, the Earth with flight paths and with a wedge cut down to the core, in front of the
  Milky Way panorama.
- Ayah cards: 2:259 (three parts), 22:47, 18:19, 18:25, 23:99–100, 23:112–115, 20:103–104, 10:45, 79:46, 97:3,
  103:1–3 — Arabic injected from the verified Uthmani text (`film/src/quran.js`, generated by
  `film/tools/mk_quran.py`); translation lines appear on the spoken words.
- No depiction of prophets, the Companions of the Cave or scholars: Uzair (AS) and the sleepers are told through
  point-of-view shots, silhouettes of places and text; scholars appear as name plates only.
- Fair-use notes are on screen where the script makes them: relativity's differences are billionths of a second and
  2:259 is a miracle, not physics; "1 ghanta 41 minute" is arithmetic, not tafsir; the Lemlich curve is a model.

**Sound**
- Music: the five trailer tracks supplied by the channel owner, cut into 34 cues (`PLAN` in `audio/mix.py`). Their big
  moments are lined up with the picture — the first build lands on "Woh jhoot nahi bol raha", the opening hit on
  "Saal 2010" and "Saal 1971", the swell of the dramatic teaser on the title, on ayah 22:47, on 309.2, on "Wohi sawal.
  Teesri baar.", on Surah Al-Asr and on "Aap KHUD waqt hain", the last hit on the timer reveal. The bed sits 7.5 dB
  under the open level while the voice speaks, and the music cuts out completely (`DROPS`) before the biggest lines
  and for the whole of the bet's silence.
- 413 sound-design hits (trailer horns, booms, whooshes, risers, ticking clocks, heartbeats, chimes, calculator keys,
  paper…) synthesised in `audio/sfx.py` — no samples, nothing to licence.
- Master: −14 LUFS integrated, −1 dBTP.

## Rebuild
Same working directory as Video 1 (`S=` at the top of the shell scripts): `v14/` holds this film's `vo/`, `edit/`,
`audio/`, `out/`; `film14/` is `film/` from this folder; `frames/`, `gfx/`, `music/wav/`, `quran/` are shared with
Video 1 (`../01-kainat-ka-pehla-lamha/assets-manifest.json`, `film/tools/frames.sh`, `film/getfonts.py`). Python
scripts run from `v14/`. Needs Python 3 (`numpy scipy soundfile noisereduce pyloudnorm rapidfuzz pillow faster-whisper
clearvoice`), Node with `playwright`, `three`, `gsap`, and `ffmpeg`.

```bash
python3 edit/vad.py                      # speech islands
python3 edit/asr_each.py 0 1             # transcribe every island on its own
python3 edit/spoken.py                   # script lines as actually spoken (MOD / ADD / DROP)
python3 edit/mk_each.py && python3 edit/dp_align.py && python3 edit/select.py   # align + choose latest takes
python3 edit/assemble.py && python3 edit/ai_sr.py && python3 edit/enhance2.py && python3 edit/make_cues.py
python3 edit/verify.py                   # after transcribing vo/final.wav: repeats / gaps report
cd ../film14 && node check.js && node export_sfx.js && cd ../v14 && python3 audio/mix.py
cd ../film14 && ./render_all.sh 0 737.97 film 4 30 && ./finish.sh ../v14/out/film_video.mp4 ../v14/audio/master.wav ../v14/out/final.mp4 21
```

Rendering is frame-by-frame in headless Chromium (software WebGL): about 45 minutes for the whole film on 4 cores.
