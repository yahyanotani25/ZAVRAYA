## Quran text

Uthmani text from Tanzil via api.alquran.cloud, inserted by script (`film/src/quran.js` is generated by
`film/tools/mk_quran.py`, never hand-typed). Single words shown on their own (e.g. كَمْ لَبِثْتُمْ) are cut out of the
same verified ayah text by word index.

a miracle, not an example of physics; "70 years = 1 hour 41 minutes" is arithmetic on the scale of 22:47, not tafsir;
the bazaar scene comes from the tafsir narrations, the Quran itself only mentions the silver coin.
